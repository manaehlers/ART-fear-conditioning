# Anxiety-Related Traits (ARTs) and Fear Conditioning

This repository contains the R analysis and visualization code accompanying the study:

**“Anxiety-Related Traits Are Associated with Subjective but Not Physiological or Neural Indices of Associative Threat Learning”**

The study investigates associations between anxiety-related traits (ARTs) and associative threat learning across acquisition, extinction, and renewal.

## Repository contents

The scripts are intended to be used in the following order:

* **`01_ARTs_analyses.R`** – Analyses of anxiety-related traits and their associations with threat-learning outcome measures.
* **`02_ARTs_plots.R`** – Visualization of anxiety-related trait distributions and associations with threat-learning outcome measures.
* **`03_conditioning_effects_analyses.R`** – Analyses of overall conditioning effects across experimental phases and outcome measures.
* **`04_conditioning_effects_plots.R`** – Visualization of conditioning effects across experimental phases and outcome measures.

## Requirements

Analyses were conducted in **R**. Required R packages are specified at the beginning of each script.
